ECHOES OF THE MOUNTAIN

A story rich fantasy adventure inspired by Indian folklore and Hindu mythology where music is your magic and choices shape your fate. Journey through desi myth and modern mischief as you cast sound spells, solve melody puzzles, and follow branching paths toward Insanity or Nirvana. Every verse you wield reshapes the world encounters, allies, and rewards bend to your rhythm inviting you to find harmony, unleash chaos, or compose a destiny that's entirely your own.
